This is sample text to show the reading experience on a story page. Story text is set in Literata, a typeface designed for long reading on screens, at a size and line length meant to feel like a letter more than a web page.

Paragraphs stay short and the margins stay generous. Nothing moves, nothing pops up, and the only color on the page comes from the tags, the links, and the Scripture.
