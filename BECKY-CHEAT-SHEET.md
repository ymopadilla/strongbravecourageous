# Your site, in plain English — Strong. Brave. Courageous.

**Where to sign in:** strongbravecourageous.com/admin/
**Your login:** the email you were invited with + the password you chose. (Forgot it? Use "Forgot password" on the login screen.)

Everything below happens in the editor. No code, ever.

## Write a story
1. Click **Stories** → **New Story**.
2. Fill in the title, the date, the categories, and a one- or two-sentence excerpt in your own words (it shows on the story card and in search).
3. Write the story in the big box. To add a photo, click the **+** (or image icon) in the toolbar. To add a video, paste a YouTube or Vimeo link on its own line.
4. Top right: **Save**. Then change the status from *Draft* to *In review*, then *Ready*, then click **Publish**. The site updates itself in about a minute.

When you publish, two things happen on their own: the story becomes searchable on the Stories page, and your email subscribers get a note with the story preview and a "Read the full story" link (sent by Mailchimp within a day).

### Categories are labels, not folders
A story lives once in the feed, in date order, and can carry more than one label. Pick every one that's true — usually one to three.

- **Grief** — the loss itself, the ache, the days that are heavy.
- **Healing** — what is mending, slowly, right now.
- **Faith** — where God shows up, what Scripture is carrying you.
- **Perseverance** — the keep-going stories, one foot in front of the other.
- **Humor** — the moments that made you laugh anyway.

Readers can tap any label (or several) on the Stories page and see every story with that label.

### Scripture links itself
Write Bible references the way you'd say them — *Joshua 1:9*, *Ps 23*, *1 Corinthians 13:4-7*, *John 3:16, 17*. The site finds each one, turns it into a link to the verse (NIV on BibleGateway), lists it on the story card, and adds it to the **Scripture** page. Nothing to fill in. One note: write **Joshua** in full for the book — "Josh" on this site means Josh, not the book.

### Years
The Stories page groups older stories by year. This year is open; earlier years fold up until a reader opens them. Every story stays searchable — nothing is archived.

## Add a resource
Click **Resources** → **New Resource**. One entry per item. The site sorts everything for you (books and songs A–Z, podcast episodes newest first under their show, articles A–Z under their publication) — you never order anything by hand.

1. **Type** — Book, Music, Podcast, Publication, or Professional Help (this picks the tab).
2. **Title** — the book, song, episode, article, or service name.
3. **Author / Artist / Show / Publication** — who it's by. For podcasts and publications, this is also the group it's listed under.
4. **Date** — release date. Needed for podcasts and publications (anything older than ten years folds into "Older" at the bottom of that tab).
5. **Link** — the web address, if there is one.
6. **Note** — one line on why it helps. Optional.
7. **Show on site** — untick to hide something without deleting it.

Then **Publish**. Readers can suggest resources through the Contact form; nothing goes on the site until you add it here.

## Fix your About, Newsletter, or Contact page
Click **Site Pages** → pick the page → edit → **Publish**. Your photo and social links live here too. The Contact page shows the form only — no email address.

## When someone shares a Fingerprint or leaves a Comment
You'll get an email from Netlify with what they wrote. Nothing shows on the site until you approve it:
1. In the editor, click **Fingerprints** (or **Comments**) → **New**.
2. Paste in their name and words. For a comment, pick which story it belongs to.
3. Tick **Approved — show on the site** → **Publish**.

**Fingerprint rule:** each submission says who it was for — *Share it with everyone* or *Just for Becky*. Only approve the ones marked **Share it with everyone**. "Just for Becky" stays between you and them.

Don't want something on the site? Do nothing. It stays private in your Netlify inbox.
