I’m Becky. I’m Steve’s wife, and I’m Mason and Josh’s mom.

I write here in celebration of the three of them. Loving them is the best part of my story, and missing them is the hardest. Both are true every day.

These stories are how I keep walking. Some are heavy, some are funny, and all of them are honest. In each one, I’m looking for where God is in it, because He keeps showing up.

I’m not a counselor, and I don’t have this figured out. I’m still here, and I’m still healing, one foot in front of the other. If you’re walking through loss too, I’m glad you found this place.
