Fingerprints or handprints are pieces of evidence establishing proof of the presence of an individual. Scripture talks about God’s handiwork and handprints so to speak.

Psalm 139:5 (NIV) says, “You hem me in behind and before, and you lay your hand upon me.” David is speaking to God. It’s God’s hand laid upon David.

Psalm 8:3 says, “When I consider your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars, which you have set in place.”

Isaiah 64:8 says, “Yet you, Lord, are our Father. We are the clay, you are the potter; we are all the work of your hand.”

In my stories, I try to share how God’s presence in my life has been evidenced by circumstances, connections, memories that only He could weave together. With eyes and ears wide-open, through prayer, I can see God’s handprints or fingerprints in my life. I am the clay; He is the potter. He continues to mold me and show me how much He cares for me which in turn turns my heart towards Him more and more. I can prove it, and I bet you can too. Courageous. readers on this fingerprints page. Either way, thank you in advance for sharing your fingerprint story to inspire others.
